# The 8 Sigmas

| Sigma | Name | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sources & Collection | Where does information come from? |
| 2 | Inventory & Knowledge | What exists? |
| 3 | Observability | What is happening? |
| 4 | Workflow Automation | What should happen automatically? |
| 5 | Signals & Confidence | What can be inferred safely? |
| 6 | Blue Camel | How should execution run? |
| 7 | Context Intelligence | What does the data mean? |
| 8 | LIA | What should happen next? |
