# Governance Model

## Core Rules

Always:

- preserve evidence
- preserve rollback safety
- preserve resumability
- prefer deterministic behavior
- preserve architecture consistency

Never:

- expand uncontrolled scope
- invent implementation
- skip validation
- merge uncertainty blindly

---

## Stop Conditions

Stop when:

- evidence becomes inconsistent
- architecture becomes unclear
- rollback becomes unsafe
- lineage breaks
- risk increases unexpectedly
